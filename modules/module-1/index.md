## 2. The Intelligence Analysis Technical Curriculum

How does IA 342 connect with the rest of your courses? Our technical curriculum is designed as a logical pipeline:

- **[IA 340] Data Mining and Analytics:** Focuses on how to **collect, organize, and analyze data** computationally. 
- **[IA 342] Visualization Methods (This Course!):** Focuses on how to **use Business Intelligence (BI) and visualization to help people understand data.**
- **[IA 343] Interactive and AI Analytics:** Focuses on how to **use interactive and AI approaches to help people and AI work together around data.**

In IA 342, our ultimate goal isn't just drawing charts; it's facilitating human understanding.

---

## 3. The Core Question: Why Visualization?

With the rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (like ChatGPT, Claude, and advanced data-crunching models), you might wonder: 

> **If AI can analyze data for us, why do humans still need visualization?**

**The Answer:** 
Because **humans still make decisions**. AI can summarize text or run regressions, but when it comes to high-stakes intelligence, human analysts and policymakers bear the responsibility of action. 

Visualization is the bridge between complex data and human cognition. It supports:
- **Human Perception:** Helping us see outliers, trends, and patterns instantly.
- **Judgment:** Allowing decision-makers to evaluate context and nuance that AI might miss.
- **Communication:** Telling a compelling, undeniable story backed by data.
- **Accountability:** Proving *why* a conclusion was reached, rather than relying on an AI's "black box."

## 4. Fall 2026 Roadmap

Rather than a rigid week-by-week calendar, our course flows through these major technical phases:

1. **ArcGIS & Spatial Visualization (First):** We start with spatial intelligence. You will learn map design, geographical contexts, and how to visualize "where" things happen.
2. **Tableau & Business Intelligence (Main Block):** This is the core engine of the course. We will spend an uninterrupted block mastering Tableau to connect data and build exploratory visuals.
3. **Mini Project:** A milestone to synthesize your early spatial and BI skills.
4. **Visual Analytics & Dashboard Design:** Moving beyond basic charts into calculated fields, interactive filters, and cohesive dashboard layouts.
5. **Final Project:** Your opportunity to build an end-to-end data story and comprehensive visual analytics application.
