IA342 · Classroom activity

Hue, value, saturation

Hold two controls fixed; change only the third. Describe the visual change before looking at the numbers.

HSV is a useful control model, not a perceptually uniform color space. Equal V does not guarantee equal perceived brightness across hues.

Color-component background: Borden D. Dent, Jeffrey S. Torguson and Thomas W. Hodler (2009), Cartography: Thematic Map Design, 6th ed., McGraw-Hill, “Color and Design of Thematic Maps”, “Components of Color” and color models. Original HSV demonstration; HSV value is not perceptual lightness. This is not a CVD simulation.
