How much larger is 100 than 94?

Three groups have 94, 96, and 100 records. Only the visible starting point of the bars changes.

Discuss the result

The values 94 and 100 differ by 6. With a zero baseline, the ratio of bar lengths is 100/94 ≈ 1.064. Starting at 90 changes the visible lengths to 4 and 10: a ratio of 2.5. Correct labels do not repair the misleading length comparison. This is a rule about bars—not a requirement that every line chart or scatter plot start at zero.
