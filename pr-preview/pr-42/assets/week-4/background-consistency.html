IA342 · Classroom activity

Same gray, different background

Are the two center squares the same color? Reveal a connecting strip, then make the backgrounds consistent.

Compare maps and charts on consistent backgrounds. Keep one category’s color consistent across views.

Original simultaneous-contrast demonstration. Related background: Borden D. Dent, Jeffrey S. Torguson and Thomas W. Hodler (2009), Cartography: Thematic Map Design, 6th ed., McGraw-Hill, “Color and Design of Thematic Maps”, color context and constancy. Both patches remain #888888; this is a schematic teaching illustration.
