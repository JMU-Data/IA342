Additive complements

A primary plus the other two primaries gives white light: red + cyan, green + magenta, blue + yellow.

In this screen model, cyan, magenta and yellow are the RGB secondary colors; they are the CMY subtractive primaries.

Concepts: Borden D. Dent, Jeffrey S. Torguson and Thomas W. Hodler (2009), Cartography: Thematic Map Design, 6th ed., McGraw-Hill, “Color and Design of Thematic Maps”, “Color Theories.” Original HTML illustration of RGB additive light and ideal CMY subtractive filters; it is not an exact physical-paint model.
