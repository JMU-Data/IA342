Illustrative data · four observation dates

Equal space does not always mean equal time.

The four observations are on days 1, 2, 11, and 31. Each step increases the measured value by 10.

Discuss the result

The intervals are 1, 9, and 20 days. The rates are therefore 10, about 1.11, and 0.5 units per day. Equally spaced category labels conceal this difference when the reader interprets the slope as change over elapsed time. Use numerical coordinates when slope is meant to represent change per unit of elapsed time.
