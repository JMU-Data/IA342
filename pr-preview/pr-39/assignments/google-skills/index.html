IA 342: Visualization Methods, Technologies, and Tools for Intelligence Analysis

Google Skills — Hands-on Lab Assignment

IA342 · Fall 2026 · Google AI Skills (10% of course grade)

Deadline: Saturday, December 12, 2026, at 11:59 PM Eastern Time. All qualifying labs must be completed and passed, and your evidence screenshot must be uploaded to Canvas, before this deadline. No late submissions are accepted.

What to do

Complete up to three or more distinct hands-on Labs on Google Skills. You may choose any Lab activities available through your account or course program. You earn partial credit for one or two passed Labs; three or more passed Labs earn full credit for this 10% course component.

Only activities whose Format/Type is Lab count. Courses, Learning Paths, checks, games, and other formats do not count—even if they are shown as completed. If a Course contains a Lab, only the individual passed Lab counts. Repeating the same Lab does not count as another distinct Lab.

Step 1 — Find eligible Labs

  1. Sign in to Google Skills with the account you use for this course.
  2. If your account has a course Programs area, you may start there to find activities offered through the program. You may also browse the Catalog.
  3. In Catalog → Format, select Lab only. Make sure Course and Learning Path are not selected.
  4. Choose a Lab, open it, select Start, and complete the hands-on work. Make sure the activity finishes with a Passed result.

Example 1 — Catalog: select Lab as the only eligible format.

A Certificate or Skill Badge is not required for this assignment. Individual Labs often record Passed status without awarding a certificate; completing a Lab does not automatically produce a certificate.

Step 2 — Verify your completed Labs

  1. In Google Skills, click your profile picture at the top right and select Progress.
  2. Select the Lab activity filter if needed.
  3. Confirm that each activity you want to count is listed as Type: Lab and has a checkmark under Passed.
  4. Keep the lab names, completion dates, and Passed results visible for your screenshot.

A 100% score is not required. For example, a Lab with a 95% score and Passed checkmark counts.

Step 3 — Submit ONE screenshot in Canvas

Before the deadline, upload one clear PNG or JPG screenshot to this Canvas assignment. It must show all of the following in the same image:

  • Your full name, visible by opening your Google Skills profile menu.
  • The names of the distinct Labs you completed (up to three needed for full credit).
  • Type: Lab and the Passed checkmark for each counted Lab.
  • The Date finished column so completion can be checked against the deadline.

If necessary, zoom out in your browser so your name and all three Lab rows fit in the same screenshot while remaining readable. Upload the screenshot directly to Canvas; do not submit a Google Skills URL, a Certificate, or a separate report. Only one screenshot is required.
