# Lab 1: System Access and Account Setup

**Due Date:** Canvas is the authoritative source for the exact due date/time and submission status.  
**Submission Location:** Canvas

## Objective

Our first technical module focuses on setting up the necessary platforms for the course, including spatial visualization using ArcGIS and AI tools via Google Cloud. This assignment is to ensure that every student has proper access to the required applications to avoid issues later in the semester.

## Instructions

### Part 1: ArcGIS Business Analyst Access Check

1. Go to: [https://jmu.maps.arcgis.com/](https://jmu.maps.arcgis.com/)
2. Sign in using the James Madison University account / JMU SSO.
3. Complete institutional MFA if required.
4. Open the ArcGIS Online App Launcher.
5. Locate ArcGIS Business Analyst.
6. Open Business Analyst.
7. Confirm whether it loads successfully without an access/license error.

*If Business Analyst does not appear or you encounter a license/access error, you do not need to troubleshoot the permissions yourself. Simply report the failure in Canvas so we can provision your account.*

### Part 2: Google Account & AI Tools Setup

*(Note: If you have already completed these steps in IA 340 this semester, you must still ensure your JMU Google account details are submitted for this course.)*

1. **Create a Google Account (Required)**: You must create/register a Google account using your official **James Madison University email address** (e.g., `@dukes.jmu.edu` or `@jmu.edu`). This allows your JMU email to access Google Cloud services and is strictly required for the instructor to grant you access to Google Cloud credits and enroll you in the Google AI Skills program.
2. **Activate Gemini Student Plan (Recommended)**: We recommend activating the free Gemini One Year Student Plan at [https://gemini.google/students/](https://gemini.google/students/) using your JMU email account. This provides access to advanced AI capabilities (Gemini Advanced / Google AI Pro tier) to assist your analytical and visualization workflow.  
   > **Important Reminder:** Activating the Gemini student plan is **optional/recommended**, not mandatory. If you choose to activate it, note that it is free for **one year only**. Please set a reminder to cancel it before or after the one-year trial ends to prevent any unwanted charges.
3. **Google AI Skills Enrollment (Required)**: Once you submit your JMU-associated Google email, the instructor will enroll you in the **Google AI Skills** program and provide access to the shared Google Cloud credits pool.

## What to Submit on Canvas

Once you have completed the steps above, go to the corresponding assignment in **Canvas** and submit your response as a text entry in the following format:

**SUCCESS:**
- **Status:** Yes. (Confirming ArcGIS access and Google account creation with JMU email; note if Gemini was activated.)
- **Google Email:** [Provide your official JMU email address used for your Google Account. This is required so the instructor can enroll you in the Google AI Skills program and Google Cloud pool.]

**FAILURE:**
- **Status:** No
- **Details:** Brief description of which required step failed (e.g., ArcGIS license error, JMU Google account creation error) + screenshot if useful.

*Note: Do not include credentials, MFA codes, or sensitive information in your screenshot. No student data, screenshots, or credentials should ever be committed to or shared in this public GitHub repository.*
