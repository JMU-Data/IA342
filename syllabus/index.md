## Course Description

IA 342 focuses on Data Visualization, Business Intelligence, Visual Analytics, and Data Storytelling. While AI can analyze data for us, **humans still make decisions**; visualization supports human perception, judgment, communication, and accountability. Throughout this course, we will use industry-standard tools (ArcGIS, Tableau) to transform raw data into interactive dashboards and compelling visual narratives.

## Course Policies

### Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy
AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) may be used to assist with your workflow (e.g., generating ideas, troubleshooting calculations, learning concepts). However, **students remain entirely responsible** for the final visual design, data interpretation, and verification of all outputs. You must ensure the accuracy and integrity of your work.

### Grading Breakdown
- **Attendance:** 20%
- **Labs:** 50%
- **Mini Project:** 10%
- **Final Project:** 20%

### Late Work & Resubmission
- Late submissions will incur a standard penalty unless prior arrangements are made or an emergency occurs.
- Resubmissions for specific assignments may be permitted at the instructor's discretion to encourage mastery of the material.

### Attendance
Regular attendance is critical. A significant portion of the learning happens in class through hands-on practice, critique, and discussion. Please notify the instructor via email *before* class if you are unable to attend due to illness or emergency.

### Academic Integrity
All students are expected to abide by the JMU Honor Code. Plagiarism, including presenting unverified AI-generated content as your own original analysis without disclosure or responsibility, is a violation of the Honor Code.

### Accessibility and Support
JMU is committed to creating a welcoming and accessible environment. If you have a documented disability and require accommodations, please register with the Office of Disability Services (ODS) and contact the instructor early in the semester to discuss your needs.

## Tentative Course Roadmap

The course is organized into high-level modules rather than a rigid weekly schedule, allowing flexibility for deep dives into core tools:

1. **Module 1 — Course Introduction and Access Check**
   - Setting up accounts and exploring the core question: *Why visualization?*
2. **ArcGIS / Spatial Visualization**
   - Map design and spatial intelligence.
3. **Tableau / Business Intelligence (BI)**
   - Connecting data, building primary charts, and exploratory data analysis.
4. **Mini Project / Checkpoint**
   - Synthesizing early spatial and BI skills.
5. **Visual Analytics and Dashboards**
   - Advanced calculations, interactivity, and dashboard design.
6. **Final Project**
   - End-to-end data storytelling and comprehensive visual analytics application.
